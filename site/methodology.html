METHODOLOGY / EVIDENCE / LIMITS

From contract prices to a Fed path

The market supplies meeting-by-meeting marginals. Everything that connects those meetings is a model. This note separates the two, shows the arithmetic, and reports what a small historical replay can—and cannot—establish.

YES PRICES→MEETING MARGINALS→ACTION LEVELS→JOINT MODEL→RATE FAN
01

WHAT IS OBSERVED

Five binary contracts become one meeting distribution

A Polymarket meeting event is not a single five-way security. It contains five separate Yes/No contracts: at least a 50 bp cut, a 25 bp cut, no change, a 25 bp hike, and at least a 50 bp hike. We prefer a valid CLOB midpoint and use the contemporaneous Gamma Yes price only when the book fails the quality gate.

Because separate Yes prices need not add to exactly one dollar, we use multiplicative normalization—also called proportional de-vigging:

pi = qi / Σqq = observed Yes price · p = displayed meeting probability

The raw sum, source, maximum spread, timestamp, liquidity, and any fallback remain visible. This convention is transparent, but it is not the only possible de-vigging rule and it does not remove fees, slippage, or settlement risk.

02

PRICE TO POLICY RATE

Actions are incremental; rate levels are cumulative

Each meeting probability is attached to a representative action of −50, −25, 0, +25, or +50 basis points. The two open-ended tails are capped at ±50 bp for arithmetic. The expected action is the probability-weighted average. Adding those actions to the current target-range upper bound produces the meeting-action path.

E[Δrt] = Σ pi,taiExpected incremental action at meeting t
E[rT] = r0 + Σt≤TE[Δrt]Cumulative meeting-action level through T

The chart targets the upper bound of the Federal funds target range, not EFFR. The current upper bound and EFFR are sourced from the New York Fed; the baseline spread is not assumed constant when the official target changes.

03

WHAT IS MODELED

Marginals do not identify a path

Meeting markets tell us the probability of each action at each date, but not questions such as “if September hikes, what happens to October?” The current tree starts with an assumed persistence kernel: similar policy stances receive more joint weight, and that influence decays with meeting distance. No constraint pulls those discrete paths toward the separately traded year-end rate distribution.

Iterative proportional fitting then adjusts the joint weights until every quoted meeting marginal is recovered. This guarantees meeting-marginal consistency; it does not make the conditional transitions observed or uniquely identified.

The fan computes quantiles across complete meeting-action paths. A selected orange branch is always jointly realizable on the modeled −50/−25/0/+25/+50 bp lattice. The connected cyan medians are pointwise quantiles and need not themselves form one realized path.

04

TWO MARKET VIEWS

The December action market and year-end rate market can disagree

The cumulative meeting-action path and the 15-bucket year-end rate distribution are independently traded. Preserving both does not make their levels economically identical. The site displays their difference as a cross-market gap—not as an extra Fed move between the December meeting and year-end.

The site deliberately does not force a reconciliation. It keeps both quotes intact so that disagreement remains visible rather than silently moving either market or inventing a fractional policy action.

WHAT THE ARCHIVE SUPPORTS

In this small reconstructed sample, probability assigned to the realized outcome generally increased as meetings approached.

WHAT IT DOES NOT SUPPORT

A conclusion that Polymarket is globally well calibrated, has proven superior resolution, or outperforms Fed funds futures.

06

REPLAY PROVENANCE

Each frame is an information set, not hindsight

Historical frames are reconstructed from Polymarket CLOB price history at a daily cadence and labeled as reconstructed. Contemporary refreshes are archived several times per day. A contract never appears before its first observable quote. When a shorter intraday collection omits an otherwise observable horizon meeting, the last prior quote may be carried forward and is visibly marked.

The realized Fed path is overlaid only after the forecast is built. Model-version changes are marked in the replay because historical and current joint-dependence regimes are not directly comparable.

Important limitations
  • Only a small, one-sided set of resolved meetings is available, with no resolved hikes in the current evaluation sample.
  • Open-ended action and rate tails use boundary representatives, which understates tail variance.
  • Kernel parameters are scenario choices; sensitivity can be economically meaningful.
  • No contemporaneous Fed funds futures benchmark is yet included.
  • Quoted probabilities omit fees, spread, slippage, settlement risk, and venue-specific participation effects.
