03 METHOD & LIMITS
Quoted

A valid CLOB midpoint is preferred; a contemporaneous Gamma price is a labeled fallback. Five Yes prices are normalized to 100%.

Archived

Each frame uses only observations available by that timestamp. No contract appears before its first quote; held or reconstructed observations are labeled.

Translated

Open-ended tails use ±50 bp for path arithmetic. The chart shows the upper bound of the target range, not EFFR or a fee-adjusted break-even.

Modeled

All 5N action paths are enumerated exactly and raked back to quoted meeting marginals. Cross-meeting persistence is assumed, not historically estimated.
